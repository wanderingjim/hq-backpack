HQ Backpack - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

HQ Backpack is a personal backup tool used only by its owner to copy their own files into their own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file permission, which only lets it see and manage files that it created itself. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.

What it does with data

Files are uploaded directly from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive. HQ Backpack does not send data to any other server, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use data for advertising or analytics.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The owner can remove access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/wanderingjim/hq-backpack.
